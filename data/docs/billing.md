# Billing

## Plans

- **Starter:** $0/month. 100 build minutes, 10 GB bandwidth.
- **Pro:** $49/month per seat. 2,000 build minutes, 500 GB bandwidth, SSO/SAML.
- **Enterprise:** custom annual pricing. Dedicated support and audit logs.

## Overage

Bandwidth overage is billed at $0.02 per GB on all plans.

## Invoices

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month. Update your payment method under
Settings > Billing. Failed payments retry automatically for 7 days before the
account is paused.
